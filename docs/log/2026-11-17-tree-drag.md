# Tree: drag and drop with drop states and caller drop rules

Id: 2026-11-17-tree-drag

`Grille-83g.14`. `Tree` takes `dragAndDropHooks` (React Aria's `useDragAndDrop` result, passed through), so
the caller's `getDropOperation` holds the drop rules and a refused drop shows no indicator. New exports:
`TreeDragHandle` (`aria-label`; a React Aria Button with `slot="drag"` and the grip icon, for TreeSection's
`drag` slot or a TreeCard's content) and `TreeDropIndicator` (`target`, `child?`; the 2px control-accent line,
for `renderDropIndicator`, `child` indents it to card level). States in `ui.css`: `data-dragging` at 50%
opacity, `data-drop-target` on a section row gets the hover fill and a 1px accent outline. The line styles
also match React Aria's default `.react-aria-DropIndicator`, so a caller with no `renderDropIndicator` gets
an unindented line. Decisions: the grip is faint until the row is hovered, as in Aurora's mock. Section
drags do not collapse the sections (Aurora does that in its own `onDragStart`, which the caller can copy).

Fixture "Drag and drop" in `src/Tree.fixture.tsx`: reorder cards within and across sections, drop a card on a
section row to append (also the way into the empty section), reorder sections, and cards refuse a drop
before or after a section (the top level). The other fixture now uses `TreeDragHandle` without hooks.
Tests (`src/ui.test.tsx`, "Tree drag and drop"): handles named by label and axe, a handle without hooks,
keyboard drag start (virtual click on the handle) renders `ui-tree-drop-child` indicators and `data-dragging`.

Could not be tested in jsdom: moving between targets and dropping (no layout for drop-target focus), the
refused-drop no-indicator case, and all visuals. Touch and iOS are Aurora's checks.
